=== ORDEN RECOMENDADO DE EJECUCIÓN ===
========================================================================
1. Entrenar todos (8 modelos):
   - W2: 1a_1, 1a_2, 1c_1, 1c_2
   - W3: 1a_1, 1a_2, 1c_1, 1c_2

2. Comparar tc=0 (sin banda):
   - W2/1a_1 vs BS, W2/1c_1 vs LRM MC
   - W3/1a_1 vs BS, W3/1c_1 vs LRM MC

3. Comparar tc=1% sin banda:
   - W2/1a_2 vs BS, W2/1c_2 vs LRM MC
   - W3/1a_2 vs BS, W3/1c_2 vs LRM MC

4. Comparar tc=1% con banda (requiere que tc=0 compare ya exista):
   - W2/1a_2 vs BS banded, W2/1c_2 vs LRM MC banded
   - W3/1a_2 vs BS banded, W3/1c_2 vs LRM MC banded

5. (Opcional) Recalibrar bandas si los resultados lo justifican.
